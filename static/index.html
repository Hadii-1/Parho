Why Parho

Your PDFs, turned into a tutor.

Everything runs around a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline, a fancy way of saying Parho finds the exact right lines in your notes before it answers, so replies stay grounded in your material.

Grounded in your notes

Answers are built from the chunks Parho retrieves from your own PDF, not guessed from thin air. Every reply lists its sources.

Remembers the thread

Ask "explain that more" and Parho knows what "that" was. It keeps the conversation in mind so follow-up questions just work.

Private per user

Each account gets its own isolated vector store, so your documents are only ever searched for you, and never mixed with anyone else's.

How it works

Three steps to smarter studying.

  1. 01

    Upload a PDF

    Drop in your lecture notes, a chapter, or a research paper. Parho reads it, splits it into passages, and turns each one into a searchable embedding.

  2. 02

    Ask a question

    Type anything in plain language. Parho retrieves the most relevant passages from your document and hands them to the AI as context.

  3. 03

    Learn with sources

    You get a clear answer plus the snippets it leaned on — so you can trust it, verify it, and dig deeper.
